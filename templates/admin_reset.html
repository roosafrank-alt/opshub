Reset Data

Master-admin only. Each button below permanently deletes the data it names — there's no undo, so double-check before confirming. Everything else in the app is left alone.

Shop
Activity Log

Clears the scan/transaction history ({{ counts.activity_log }} entries). Current stock counts are not affected — this only wipes the log of how they got there.

Orders

Deletes all pending/received/cancelled orders ({{ counts.orders }} orders). Parts and stock levels are not affected.

Projects & Numbering

Deletes all projects ({{ counts.projects }}), their sections, labor sessions, and photos. This also resets project numbering back to 001 for the current year, since the next number is based on the highest one in use. Part transaction/order history is kept, just un-linked from the deleted project.

Flight School
Flight Log

Deletes all logged flights ({{ counts.flights }}) for every plane. Planes, students, instructors and bookings are kept, and student balances don't change.

Schedule

Deletes every booking on the schedule ({{ counts.schedule }}), including pending requests, denied and cancelled ones. Flights already logged are kept.

Squawks

Clears the squawk flag on {{ counts.squawks }} flight(s) currently flagged. The flight log entries themselves are kept — only the "needs shop attention" flag is cleared.

Student Accounts & Balances

Clears every student's account history ({{ counts.ledger }} entries: funds added, flight charges, adjustments) and sets every balance back to $0. Students and flights are kept.

Students

Deletes all students ({{ counts.students }}) with their logged flights, bookings and account history. Logins that were only for a student are removed too; logins that also have shop access are kept. Station accounts (like "Shop") are kept.

Instructors

Deletes all instructor profiles ({{ counts.instructors }}) except master admins' own. Their logged flights are kept with no instructor listed, and their upcoming bookings are kept but flagged "needs review" so you can pick a new instructor.

Flight School Planes

Deletes all Flight School planes ({{ counts.planes }}), along with their flights, bookings, maintenance items/log, to-dos, compression checks and plane photos. Any shop project that referenced one of these planes is kept, just un-linked.

Reset All Flight School Data

Does every Flight School reset above in one go: schedule, flight log, account balances, students, instructors (except master admins' own) and planes, plus queued phone alerts and the live-map trail. Shop data (parts, orders, projects, shop aircraft) and master admin logins are not touched. Type RESET in the box to turn the button on.
