Shutting the Pi down

OpsHub is going offline for everyone until the Pi is turned back on.

  1. Wait until the Pi's green light stops blinking (about 20-30 seconds). The red light may stay on - that's fine.
  2. Now it's safe to unplug the power.
  3. To turn it back on, plug the power back in (or press the small power button once on a Raspberry Pi 5). OpsHub comes back by itself in about a minute.

This page can't reach the Pi once it's off, so there is nothing more to do here. The uptime monitor will send its "OpsHub is down" alert while it's off - that's expected.
